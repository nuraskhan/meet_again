# PLAN 020: plans as lines in the calendar; photographs as default pictures

1. Calendar cell: the plans of a day are no longer a dot with a number beside the date. Under the
   date stands one soft line per plan, in the colour of the plan's activity; with more than three
   plans - their number, on a soft tint of the same colour.
2. Default pictures: a photograph per activity instead of the drawn scene. From Unsplash, under
   its licence (free to use, no credit required), bundled with the application - not loaded from
   Unsplash at run time. No recognisable people, no brand in the centre of the frame.
